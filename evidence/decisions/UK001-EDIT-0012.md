# UK001-EDIT-0012 — Paper 2 five-column schema and rosette restoration

Status: applied, deterministically built, visually checked, and independently replayed on 2026-08-14. This is a working-successor decision with no publication or DOI effect.

## Decision

Restore the Paper 2 rectangular schema from four columns to the five columns carried by project authority ED0014, restore the omitted terminal row of the upper schema, and place the same verified source rosette at the upper continuation point and beside the lower `*)` attachment marker.

The Ukrainian prose and localized lower source note remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No terminology, sentence wording, formula variable, citation, label, or cross-reference was edited.

## Considerations

The inherited display was not merely a compressed layout. It omitted a complete fifth column, the terminal continuation row, and both matching attachment marks that tell the reader how the lower fragment joins the upper schema. Those omissions materially weakened both source fidelity and intelligibility.

The repair follows ED0014 lines 606–658 and the retained printed/GDZ evidence. The rosette is the adjudicated 108×108 source-derived bitmap, used twice with the same fixed size, advance, and vertical placement. The `*)` note relationship is explicit rather than inferred from page position.

Alternatives rejected:

- Keep four columns: this preserves a known omission rather than a defensible source variant.
- Substitute a generic star: the source uses two matching non-generic rosettes.
- Redraw or smooth the glyph: the accepted bitmap already has pinned custody and placement evidence.
- Reword the Ukrainian note: its meaning is adequate, and exact preservation avoids unnecessary translation drift.
- Edit frozen v047, public r35, or standalone DOI files: this repair belongs only to the isolated successor.
